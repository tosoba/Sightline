## Question

What category set should Sightline expose for a tourist/exploration audience?

Redesign from product intent, product-first.
Drop, merge, or replace the current 31 OSM-derived categories as needed, optimized for what a tourist would search for in a new place.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-11-16.
Sightline's target taxonomy is 13 categories, designed product-first for a radial-discovery tourist audience.

Discovery:

- Sights & landmarks - monuments, landmarks, attractions, viewpoints.
- Arts & culture - museums, galleries, theatres, cinemas.
- Food & drink - restaurants, cafes, bars, fast food.
- Nightlife - nightclubs, casinos, live music.
- Shopping - malls, shops, markets.
- Nature & outdoors - parks, beaches, hiking trails.
- Library - public libraries.

Essentials:

- Accommodation - hotels, hostels.
- Getting around - transit, taxi, bike/scooter rental.
- Road trip - fuel, EV charging, parking, car rental.
- Money & banking - ATM, bank, currency exchange.
- Health - pharmacy, hospital.
- Practical - toilets.

Decisions that shaped this: radial discovery only; hybrid granularity (broad buckets plus essentials); keep all marginal areas including road trip; drop nine locals-oriented categories (CarWash, Doctors, Veterinary, CommunityCentre, FireStation, ParcelLocker, Police, PostBox, PostOffice); bars sit in Food & drink; Arts & culture is its own bucket; Library kept standalone, Bank folded into Money & banking.
